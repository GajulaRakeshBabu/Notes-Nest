## 📖 Usage Guide

### 📝 Creating Your First Note

1. **Register or Login** using your email and password.
2. Click on the **“Add New Note”** button.
3. Fill in the **Title** and **Content** fields.
4. *(Optional)* Add **Tags** such as `work`, `important`, etc.
5. *(Optional)* Attach files by clicking the **📎 paperclip icon**.
6. Hit the **“Add Note”** button to save your note!

---

### ⚙️ Advanced Features

- ✏️ **Editing Notes**: Click the **pencil icon** on any note to edit it.
- 🗑️ **Deleting Notes**: Click the **trash icon** — a confirmation will appear.
- 🔍 **Searching Notes**: Type keywords into the **search bar** to filter results in real-time.
- 📤 **Exporting Notes**: Click the **“Export Notes”** button to download your notes as a JSON backup.
- 📥 **Importing Notes**: Click the **“Import Notes”** button to restore notes from a previously exported file.
